## Selected work

Each title links to a short case study.

- **[Slow-leak detection for high-voltage breakers](https://athmanebenarous.com/work/slow-leak-detection)** · Hydro-Québec Research Institute (IREQ), 2026\
  Sensitivity 0.867 vs 0.733 for the physical baseline on synthetic leaks, at an equal false-event budget. The gain is conditional, and the model is not certified for operational use.
- **[LLM agents in production at a food plant](https://athmanebenarous.com/work/llm-agents-food-plant)** · Russet House, 2026–present\

- **[Self-learning line-speed advisory](https://athmanebenarous.com/work/line-speed-advisory)** · Russet House, 2026–present\

- **[ML pipelines over 600K+ materials](https://athmanebenarous.com/work/materials-ml-pipelines)** · Chemia Discovery, 2026\
  Pipeline runtime from 30+ hours to under 1 hour.
- **[3D neuron soma segmentation platform](https://athmanebenarous.com/work/soma-segmentation)** · McGill capstone, 2024–present · [code](https://github.com/ImagiNeuron)\
  One marker per soma (center and radius) gives its full 3D binary mask.
- **[AlphaGateau for Breakthrough](https://athmanebenarous.com/work/alphagateau-breakthrough)** · McGill, 2026 · [code](https://github.com/Otm02/alphagateau-breakthrough-v2)\
  5×5 pretraining lifted the GNN's 8×8 win rate at iteration 5 from 17% to 67% (12-game greedy evaluation).
- **[Energy profile of V-JEPA2 training](https://athmanebenarous.com/work/vjepa2-energy)** · McGill, 2026 · [code](https://github.com/Otm02/vjepa2-energy-char)\
